# D-024 · A narrow `road_loop` world in tesla_sim

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Status:** Accepted

tesla_city's roads are 21.5 m wide, so a 10 m camera never sees both edges
and anything that follows road edges (road_follower, cart_driver's road-centre
cost) can't be tested there. `worlds/road_loop.wbt` (generated by
road_follower's `tools/make_world.py`) is a 366 m loop of 7 m road with 0.15 m
curbs. It lived outside git on the Orin with the old level camera; here it has
the real cart mount (1.52 m, 9° down). Run with `world:=road_loop.wbt`.
